## Auditor's own summaries

The per-entry table above records only corrections tied to one row. Each auditor also reported findings that span the list — stale-but-working URLs, renumbering spot-checks, and which pages blocked plain `curl` and had to be verified another way. Those are reproduced verbatim.

### verify-support:food-domain-licences-ops

> All 31 entries check out: every URL resolves and every quoted string was located verbatim (byte-for-byte substring match) on the target page or in the local file. No corrections required.
> 
> Method: each remote URL was fetched twice where possible — once via WebFetch and once via raw curl with browser headers, then HTML-stripped and substring-matched against the exact quoted text. Local file:// entries were read directly from disk.
> 
> Notes that are NOT failures but worth knowing:
> 
> 1. Bot-blocked but live. Four hosts return 403 to plain curl while serving real content to a browser/WebFetch: fsis.usda.gov, www.ncbdn.org, www.w3.org (Cloudflare challenge), and ai.google.dev (302 to a Google OAuth bounce). All four were confirmed live with their quotes intact. In particular the two NCBDN slugs are real legacy per-question pages (both are search-indexed with matching titles, and the same text also appears consolidated at https://ncbdn.org/frequently-asked-questions/) — do not treat the 403 as a dead link.
> 
> 2. Entry 6 uses an http:// scheme (http://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/). It upgrades cleanly to https and returns 200 with 0 redirects; consider writing it as https:// for tidiness.
> 
> 3. Entry 7 (FDC FAQ) renders the quote with curly quotation marks — "snapshot in time," — where the analyst transcribed straight quotes. Same text, typography only.
> 
> 4. Entry 12 (FDA Step 3) truncates the page's phrase, which continues "...treatment of a disease or condition". The quoted substring is present verbatim; just be aware the sentence does not end where the quote ends.
> 
> 5. Entry 25 (SRE Being On-Call): the quote is a clause, not a full sentence. Full context: "...the minimum number of engineers needed for on-call duty from a single-site team is eight: assuming week-long shifts, each engineer is on-call (primary or secondary) for one week every month."
> 
> Supporting claims embedded in the source_name fields were also spot-checked against the repo at the repository root and all hold:
> - LICENSE.md is MIT; web/package.json line 96 is "license": "ISC" — the licence conflict claim is accurate.
> - web/node_modules web-push, axe-core and lightningcss all declare MPL-2.0; @img/sharp-libvips-darwin-arm64 declares LGPL-3.0-or-later.
> - THIRD_PARTY_LIBRARIES.md has exactly one heading, "# 📦 Third-party Dependencies (Next.js: /web)", and 53 table rows covering only /web (which itself has 38 deps + 31 devDeps), while backend/ ships its own pyproject.toml + uv.lock and data/ has requirements.txt — neither is covered. The "covers only half the project" claim is correct.
> - data/prompts/system_prompt.txt line 53 is exactly "- Estimate green_score by environmental impact (plant-based = higher)."

### verify-support:law-a11y-security

> Checked all 49 entries (48 distinct URLs — entries 27 and 28 cite the same ASVS V2 file). Every URL resolves (HTTP 200), and 47 of 49 quotes are verbatim on the cited page. Two problems: (1) Entry 44 (FSIS Food Product Dating) — a substantive misattribution: the page assigns the infant-formula safety exception to the "Use-By" date, not the "Best if Used By" date, so the quote as written contradicts the source. (2) Entry 24 (ADA web rule fact sheet) — the quoted sentence is a splice, not verbatim; the page reads "The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) Version 2.1, Level AA is the technical standard..." The substance is correct. Three URLs still work but have moved and should be updated to their canonical targets: entry 23 NEI 301s to /eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/color-blindness; entry 37 Supabase 308s to /docs/guides/getting-started/api-keys; entry 25 NCSU 301s to a trailing-slash URL. Spot-verified the renumbering-prone citations and all held: ASVS requirement numbers 2.2.1, 2.4.1 and 13.3.1 match the live V2/V13 chapters exactly (V8's IDOR text is 8.2.2, and that entry claims no number); WCAG 2.5.8 is confirmed Level AA and new in 2.2; CJEU C-184/20 is confirmed as OT v Vyriausioji tarnybinės etikos komisija, Grand Chamber, 1 Aug 2022, ECLI:EU:C:2022:601; RCW 19.373.010 is confirmed as the My Health My Data Act definitions section (2023 c 191 s 3); N.C.G.S. 75-65 and 75-61 carry the stated section headings. Three minor formatting notes, all counted clean: entry 1 capitalizes "An" because the quote starts mid-sentence (page: "means an electronic record..."); entry 38 is verbatim apart from inline code formatting around NEXT_PUBLIC_; entry 41's ellipsis joins two adjacent lines of one Pydantic code block, both present. Six W3C pages, CDC, FSIS and the Gemini terms blocked plain curl (403 / OAuth redirect) and were verified via WebFetch or full browser headers — none of those blocks indicates a bad URL.
